# 3 Aug 2026 · Work that shows its figures

**Status** verified · **PR** #65 · **Category** design

## Context

The projects were photographs with captions. An ecological garden's
argument is what *changed* — water held instead of lost, species that
came back, a soil that drains — and none of that was on the page.

## Implementation

A measured outcomes block with counted figures. A slider that wipes
between the site before and after. Client quotes attributed to the
client rather than written in the studio's own voice.

## Result

The template the studio's real projects will drop into.

**The entries in it are still placeholder fiction.** That is the largest
single thing holding the site back, and it needs the owner's own
photography — it cannot be built.
