# 2 Aug 2026 · Installed, and continuous

**Status** verified · **PRs** #57, #59–#64 · **Category** ux

## Context

Clicking a project card threw the visitor onto a new page and made them
find their place again. And the site, added to a home screen, was still
a browser tab in disguise.

## Implementation

**View transitions.** The card's own photograph becomes the case study's
hero, so the two pages are one movement rather than two states.

**App mode.** Installed, the site behaves as an app in its own right and
keeps working beside the website rather than replacing it.

**Dock refinements**, five of them: the three highlighted circles share
one stadium; back-to-top swaps in place with the menu button rather than
adding a sixth slot; the wings fold away while reading and return on an
upward flick; an even glass ring around the collapsed start button; and
the active page shows on a fresh load rather than only after a
navigation.

## Result

The clearest single piece of craft on the site, and the one visitors
notice without being able to name.
